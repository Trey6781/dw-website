Cooler Decal Kit

Broad River Boat Soda Club

If anybody asks, it’s a soda

A river-club patch built for the lid of a cooler: pines, a sunset on the water, and a tallboy standing right in the middle of it.

The artwork

Four pieces

The roundel and the crest are two takes on the same club mark — one scene-forward, one type-forward. Pick whichever your crew likes and put the other on the truck. The banner fills the long front panel, and the mini is the member sticker.

Roundel — 4.5″ circle · the lid mark
Crest — 4 × 5″ shield · carries the punchline
Banner — 6 × 2″ · front panel
Mini — 2″ BRBSC monogram · tumblers, rod tubes, back glass
In place

On the cooler

Shown on a 45-quart rotomolded cooler. Keep decals off the lid gasket, the hinge, and the latch keepers — anything that flexes or gets pried will lift a corner by the second trip.

Lid, dead center

Roundel at 4.5″. The flat recess between the handle lugs is the only truly flat real estate on a Tundra-style lid. The crest drops into the same spot at 4 × 5″.

Front panel, above the drain

Banner at 6″. Sits below the latch keepers and clear of the drain plug, so nothing rides over a seam or a moving part.

Palette

Colors that survive a summer on the water

Flat spot-style colors, no gradients or fine tints — they cut cleanly in vinyl, print consistently, and hold up when the sun bleaches everything else on the boat.

Production

Print & cut spec

ItemSpec
SizesRoundel 4.5″ dia. · Crest 4 × 5″ · Banner 6 × 2″ · Mini 2″ dia. — all measured over the white cut border.
MaterialCast vinyl (Oracal 751 / 3M IJ180) for rotomolded and curved lids; calendared (Oracal 651) is acceptable only on flat panels.
LaminateGloss UV-blocking overlaminate. Non-negotiable — unlaminated orange is the first thing to fade.
CutContour die cut on the outside of the white border, ~0.09″ (2.3 mm) wide at full size. Do not cut to the navy.
BleedArtwork already carries the border as bleed. Add 0.125″ if the printer requires it outside the cut line.
ColorBuild to the hex values above. For spot color: Navy PMS 539, Orange PMS 165, Pine PMS 7484, Teal PMS 7717, Cream PMS 7499, Coral PMS 7597.
FilesVector SVG with no external fonts — the BOAT SODA CLUB wordmark is outlined paths, so nothing substitutes at the RIP. Ring type and the tagline use a bold system face; outline those before cutting.
Durability5–7 years outdoor with laminate. Hand wash only; avoid pressure washers and dishwashers.

Surface prep

  • Wipe with 70% isopropyl, not soap — residue kills adhesion.
  • Apply above 50°F / 10°C on a fully dry lid.
  • Skip heavily pebbled panels; vinyl only holds at the high points.

Application

  • Hinge with tape down the middle, peel one half, squeegee from center out.
  • Work the die-cut edge last so no corner is left proud.
  • Give it 24 hours before the cooler sees ice or river water.

Sizing by cooler

  • 20–35 qt: mini on the lid, banner on the front.
  • 45–65 qt: roundel or crest on the lid, banner on the front.
  • 75 qt and up: roundel at 6″ — scale freely, it is vector.
